I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon Web Services (AWS) in New York, where I develop language models for AI protection and log anomaly detection. I received my Ph.D. in Machine Learning from the Georgia Institute of Technology in May 2025, advised by [Dr. Chao Zhang](http://chaozhang.org/) and [Dr. Le Song](https://www.linkedin.com/in/le-song-03223813/). My research interests are language models, uncertainty estimation, weak supervision, and information extraction.

Research Interests
---

- **Large language models**: low-rank expert adapter ensembles [[ELREA. 2025](https://openreview.net/forum?id=l0gZS0sAlf)], structured information extraction with LLMs [[G&O. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13364)], LLM-generated training data for smaller models [[ProgGen. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11103)], and the source of LLM reasoning abilities [[Minesweeper. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07387)].
- **Information extraction**: weakly supervised named entity recognition and text classification [[CHMM. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.12848), [Sparse CHMM. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14228), [Wrench. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11377), [Ren et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04582)] and HTML information extraction [[TrENC. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14549)].
- **Uncertainty estimation**: uncertainty quantification for LLM answers [[UQAC. 2025](https://openreview.net/forum?id=QTrW2HWNXe)] and for molecular property prediction [[MUBen. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10060)].
- **Text generation**: syntax-guided paraphrase generation [[GuiG. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01737)].

I received my M.S. degree from the School of ECE at Georgia Tech, where I worked with [Prof. Ying Zhang](https://zhang.ece.gatech.edu/) on Radar SCG signal processing and understanding [[Li et al. 2020](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8920010), [Xia et al. 2021](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9143413)].
